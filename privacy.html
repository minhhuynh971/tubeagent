Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What we access

With your authorization, TubeAgent uses the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API (scopes user.info.basic, video.publish) and the YouTube Data API to read your basic profile and upload videos you choose to your own account.

What we store

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer and are never sold, shared or sent to any third party. No analytics, tracking or advertising data is collected.

Third parties

Data is exchanged only with TikTok and Google/YouTube, as required to publish videos.

Deletion

You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok or Google account settings; deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
